# Welcome to FRAMily

### The International FRAM Community

**Connecting People. Navigating Complexity. Building Resilience.**

FRAMily is an international community of researchers, practitioners, 
educators, and students interested in the Functional Resonance Analysis 
Method (FRAM).

We foster open knowledge exchange, shared learning, collaboration, and 
the responsible development and application of FRAM across research, 
education, and practice.

## Explore our repositories

### FRAM Model Repository

A community collection of FRAM models, examples, and case studies from 
different application domains.

### FRAM Learning Material

Educational resources, tutorials, exercises, teaching materials, and 
training content for learning and teaching FRAM.

### FRAM Software

Software tools, applications, documentation, and digital resources 
supporting FRAM research, education, and practice.

### FRAM Resource Library

A curated collection of publications, case studies, references, and 
community resources related to FRAM and resilience engineering.
